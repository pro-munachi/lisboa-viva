The downloadable gzip-compressed geography JSON files and uncompressed
lisbon-venues.json and lisbon-locations.json make the derived database available.
Named venue and street/locality identities, coordinates, addresses and website
tags come from the recorded OSM snapshot. Street/locality focus positions may
be approximate and do not establish surveyed entrances or exact addresses.
Original decorative storefront art and simulation code do not change the
license of this derived database.
