# ROAM Lisbon pilot

The preserved `docs/PRD.md` and PR #1 establish the tourist trip simulation. The accepted ROAM design supplies the warm Lisbon palette, navy controls, trip panels and original traveller identity. The later city brief expands the world into one rotatable, draggable and interactive geographic region with Cais do Sodré, Benfica and Sintra, moving locals/vehicles and adult-proportioned characters. This supersedes the PRD's original “no 3D character system” restriction. No reference-game assets are copied.

## Delivered

The default Three.js world uses real mapped streets and footprints across Lisbon and Sintra, with public elevation data for the hills. The dataset contains **273,347 building footprints**, **123,185 roads/paths**, **16,377 surfaces** and **83 geographic chunks**. Its 86 deterministic gzip files total **19.33 MB**. Nearby chunks load detail on demand; distant buildings use a coarse geographic overview. These are rendering partitions within one coordinate space, not separate district islands.

The camera supports Rotate, Pan, zoom, Street level and Whole region views, mouse/touch gestures and labelled control buttons. Walk mode lets the traveller choose a nearby ground target. Original articulated characters use adult proportions, varied skin tones, four outfits and four hair variants. Simulated NPCs, cars and yellow trams move on local mapped paths; ambient movement can be paused. Characters provides a close view of four original outfit/hair variants.

Seventeen curated hotspots carry geographical coordinates, official cultural references and Maps links. Local landmark search and validated coordinate-link pins work without a Google key. Players can add, inspect and remove up to 16 custom pins; saved pins are revalidated from their Maps URLs. Google Places content is not scraped or bundled. An optional official Google 3D renderer is available when a restricted browser key is configured; the project currently uses open data because no key has been supplied.

The home and enterable hotspot scenes add six original imagined room kinds: home, café, gym, shop, museum/gallery and bar. Restaurants use the café scene. Eighteen free exploratory activities share exact IDs between the catalog, visible props and control panel. Floating activity icons select the same room corners as the furniture and panel; the named action button starts the activity. The city's floating category icons and room icons are original glyphs in Lisbon-inspired tile badges, with native keyboard controls and bounded screen decluttering. Enterable venue kinds come from the bundled OSM venue categories or curated museum identities; arbitrary custom pins, street/locality search results and unclassified Google Places results do not imply a business interior from a name alone.

Room actions walk the traveller around furniture on a bounded navigation grid, then use a short activity pose. Rest/table/bench/lounge actions seat the traveller on a modelled seat and return to a clear standing position afterward. Original room props, procedural Lisbon materials and one or two simulated residents provide context. Ambient pause or reduced motion freezes walking, poses and activity progress until movement resumes. Entering/exiting a room and completing these actions do not charge the trip ledger, advance its clock, change energy/meters or create journal/save records. Room activity state is temporary and clears on exit or reload.

The arrival profile adds tourist, digital nomad and expat categories, six interests, airport/station/bus-stop/home arrival styles, 1–30-day UTC calendar ranges, four accommodation styles and eight character colours. Legacy trip setups replay with a settled-home default. Dates and optional fields are validated on save replay; an unfinished calendar range cannot start a new trip. Accommodation choice shapes the profile and imagined home label without booking a real property.

The journey ledger adds a simulated transport card, one pending ticket, booking and explicit boarding. Mapped hub anchors support airport, Rossio rail and Cais bus-stop scenes. Public-transport game routes require the card. Booking checks the complete journey's affordability, time and energy before charging; a ready ticket blocks other clock-changing actions until it is boarded or the user explicitly finishes. Boarding changes destination, time and energy once. A simulated departure flight is explicitly charged and completes the chapter on boarding. Fares, transfers and times are authored, not live operator information. Profile-specific local and fellow-traveller encounter content is fictional.

Authored profile recommendations work offline. Optional server-side AI ranks only the existing chapter IDs with a strict schema and revalidation; failure or missing credentials returns the authored picks. No generated venue names, prices, openings or services enter the map. Exact dates, avatar colours, history and custom pins do not enter the AI prompt. The connection has controlled-response tests; live-key validation remains pending.

The trip game retains a euro budget; traveller style/base; **nine destinations, 45 scenarios and 90 choices**; preview/consequence; budget/time/energy rules; unique experiences; three optional challenges; passport; journal/recap from confirmed actions; private itinerary download; local save/resume; earlier runs; restart/deletion; cultural references/review dates; and activity controls that remain usable if WebGL fails.

The pilot has no persistent trip backend. Small stateless server routes resolve supported Maps links and optional AI recommendations. Repository write access and reviewed pull requests provide the restricted content publishing workflow. Server-backed accounts/editor can be added for cross-device trips or external authoring. Legacy database and APIs remain removed from the active application.

## Deliberate simplifications

- **Geographic accuracy has limits.** Footprints/streets use the OSM snapshot; heights are tagged, derived from storeys or estimated defaults. Facades, roofs and vehicle appearances are stylised. Terrain is a 193 × 193 grid and does not reproduce pavement-level slopes, steps, bridges or retaining walls. Courtyard holes and some surface holes are omitted. There is no exact architectural or photorealistic claim.
- **Actors are simulations.** Pedestrians/traffic follow short local paths, without real departures, live traffic, full traffic rules or complete city navigation/collision meshes. Free camera and room exploration do not spend trip time/money; confirmed trip choices and travel do. Outdoor walk targets are constrained near the traveller and aligned to available mapped paths, without full route planning. Interior walking routes around explicit furniture footprints with avatar clearance; it is a local room system rather than city physics.
- **Travel is illustrative.** Fixed costs/durations are not live routes/fares. Sintra is a separate municipality and uses the train option or substantially longer walk/cycle estimates; a tram cannot travel there. Overnight rest returns the trip to the chosen home neighbourhood. Accommodation has an original imagined home scene, not a surveyed hotel interior or booking.
- **Interiors are imagined.** Venue names and category/location provenance can be real while the enterable room is original artwork. Layouts, furnishings, residents, stock and activities do not establish actual business floor plans, services, access or opening status. Shop actions do not purchase items or change an inventory; music-corner activity does not promise real live audio or an event. Mirrors are decorative, not a separate real-time reflection renderer.
- **Content is authored.** Scenarios have two choices and can be completed once per trip. There is no LLM-generated venue data. Rest/overnight are free. Inspiration/connection are game meters, not a universal score or real-person counts.
- **Persistence is local.** Five trips and at most 16 custom pins remain in the same browser. Content version mismatch rejects an incompatible save visibly. Upgrade migrations remain necessary before a public service with active users.
- **Exports stay private.** Itinerary text derives from the action ledger and labels simulated/unconfirmed activities. There is no public sharing endpoint to revoke.

## Remaining before full PRD R1 sign-off

Authenticated content editor, cross-device accounts/server-backed trips, explicit revocable public sharing, verified production cost estimates, richer seasonal content, physical mobile performance measurements, full WCAG 2.2 AA audit and itinerary usefulness evaluation.

Architectural fidelity needs better facade/roof models, surveyed height detail, richer terrain and collision/navigation data. Google imagery or another licensed photogrammetry source would be a separately configured provider, subject to its coverage/terms. The open-data world remains usable without that dependency.

R2 social discovery, groups, chat, reporting/blocking and moderation remain outside this delivery. No real player presence is fabricated.
