# Research and originality

Inspected https://lagoslife.eliysites.com on 2026-10-05. Public screen shows a city map, floating venue labels, compact account controls and a sign-up gate when entering venues. No account was created there; authenticated mechanics were not inspected. Lisboa Viva uses only that general map-first premise. All SVG illustrations, names of fictional businesses, NPCs, copy and code are newly authored.

Verified geographic corridors against primary sources:
- https://www.cp.pt/info/documents/d/cp/comboios-urbanos-lisboa-cascais — Cais do Sodré, Belém and Carcavelos on the Cascais line.
- https://ttsl.pt/avisos/cacilhas-cais-do-sodre-a-partir-de-28-abr/ — Cais do Sodré–Cacilhas ferry endpoints.

Every game duration, fare, opening window and schedule is simulated. Public-service disruptions and actual timetables are not imported. The map deliberately compresses distance. Carcavelos is on the coast; Cacilhas is across the Tejo and is not a beach. Walking to the Carcavelos beach is included in the train-leg duration, rather than teleporting a ferry to the beach.
