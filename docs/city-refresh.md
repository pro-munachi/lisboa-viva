# City appearance and transport camera review

These requests extend ROAM's existing Lisbon pilot. Lagos Life is a reference for readable, playful map interactions; all models, signs and category glyphs here are original. The map continues to use the real geographic bundle rather than rearranged venue clusters.

## Request order

1. **Give buildings distinct identities.** Seven procedural façade families (historic, azulejo, townhouse, civic, modern, office and industrial), fourteen shared colour variants, four roof finishes and flat/gable/hip/mansard silhouettes. Recorded building kinds and footprint/height proportions guide the selection; untagged buildings use a stable geographic/identity seed. Source footprints and total height envelopes stay intact. Nearby balconies, cornices and chimneys regenerate around the camera in one bounded merged mesh per chunk. The far overview uses four shared roof/body prototypes instead of uniformly coloured cubes.
2. **Make hotspots colourful and inviting.** Cafés, restaurants, gyms, shops, nightlife and culture have category colours, larger 44px icon targets, named signs and distinct entry details. Street cafés have striped canopies and mixed-colour terrace seating. Selected and keyboard-focused icons have clear outlines and readable names. Trees, parks and daytime river colours are richer. These details illustrate a category; they do not claim the real business has that façade, furniture or services.
3. **Mix game and app typography.** Self-hosted Outfit gives headings, navigation and map labels a rounded character; DM Sans serves longer text, forms and chat. Both retain their SIL Open Font License files. The app makes no runtime request to Google Fonts.
4. **Show the chosen transport in the camera.** Implemented: original vehicle scenes for onboarding arrival and confirmed journeys, including airport landing, metro/train boarding and movement, bus, tram, taxi, cycling and walking. A final camera transition reveals the mapped destination. Skip and a static reduced-motion version are supported. Booking and game costs remain separate from the temporary visual sequence; completion and reload cannot charge again.
5. **Include beaches.** Implemented: 24 named beach features from the same OSM snapshot, actual coastal coordinates, a Beaches discovery filter, aqua outdoor map icons and bounded umbrellas/deck chairs on matching mapped sand. Beaches do not open a business interior. See [beach coverage and geographic limits](beaches.md).
6. **Talk to other players.** Implemented: clicking a real character, a public message author or a nearby entry opens their action card. Message opens a private conversation; the inbox groups existing conversations. The session determines the sender and the API restricts each conversation to its two participants. See [chat contract and verification](chat.md).
7. **Find and join nearby players.** Implemented: genuine arrivals produce a temporary popup, while a permanent Find nearby players control opens a venue/area directory. Join them resolves the newest live public venue and enters its original room. Other live players in that exact room appear together with their saved character appearances. Map browsing no longer publishes a false visit; public venue entry and trip arrival determine presence. See [presence behavior and boundaries](multiplayer.md).
8. **Give flights more atmosphere and detail.** Implemented: a tapered airliner, swept wings, animated engines, full landing gear, original livery and a detailed runway/apron/terminal replace the sparse flight tableau. Approach, flare, main-wheel touchdown, nose settling and rollout lead into the geographic arrival. Outside, Cabin and My seat camera views show an original cabin with the saved traveller, decorative passengers and crew. Views preserve the journey clock and do not create another booking or charge. See [transport visuals](travel-visuals.md).
9. **Give the flight a more natural finish.** A closed curved cabin, rounded upholstery, an eye-level aisle camera and a framed wing/window view replace the exposed cutaway. Original fabric, carpet, asphalt and paint maps separate the surfaces. Isolated flight lights and contact shadows retain depth; a self-hosted CC0 sky supplies daylight atmosphere and reflections. The established adult character style remains. Review the actual application screenshots and verification in [verification.md](verification.md), with asset attribution in [the travel license](../apps/web/public/textures/travel/LICENSE.txt).
10. **Create and change a character in 3D.** Start with a Male/Female character model, then customize its clothes, hair and colours. The registration editor uses the same articulated character geometry as the city, with drag-to-turn, front view and a face/hair close-up. Male offers Casual, Jacket and Explorer; Female also offers Dress. Their four stable hairstyle IDs have distinct model-specific shapes and labels, and the five server-approved colour palettes remain available. Changing models preserves compatible choices; moving from Dress to Male selects Casual in the draft. **Edit character** opens **Your wardrobe** after signup. **Save look** verifies the authenticated account and all eight returned appearance fields before updating the active city and presence appearance; Cancel discards the draft. Changing a saved look preserves the traveller's current position and room activity. The saved top colour takes precedence over the old trip palette. Browser persistence and responsive review are recorded separately in [verification.md](verification.md).
11. **Bring streets and parks to life.** Implemented: the eight categories in the supplied [NPC reference](../data/npc_demographic_categories.csv) guide original simulated people, including solo visitors, coordinated families, accompanied children, seniors, locals, fellow tourists and uniformed professionals. Different paces, short pauses and original totes, scarves, aprons, cameras, canes, strollers and staff accessories make their roles visible. Cafés gain striped esplanada parasols, cups and menu boards; mapped bar/shop fronts have warm lanterns, entrance trim and planters. Gardens gain paths along real mapped footways, benches and flowerbeds. The original mapped streets, parks and business locations stay intact. Simulated pedestrians do not represent live users, customers or employees and do not receive private messaging actions. Placement and density limits are below; browser evidence is in [verification.md](verification.md).
12. **Simplify the wardrobe and give the character more space.** A larger full-body studio sits beside the clothes, hair and colour controls on desktop. The wardrobe removes the registration editor's extra visible frame/title, uses calmer surfaces and clearer outfit cards, and keeps Save/Cancel in a footer while the editor scrolls. Phone layout stacks the body selector, a compact preview and choices in matching DOM/visual order. **Your current look** changes to **Unsaved changes** when the local draft differs; Save is available only for a changed look. A legacy Male + Dress fallback counts as an explicit draft correction, preserving the saved record until Save. The original eight-field API and cancel/error behavior are retained. Desktop, 390 × 844 phone and 844 × 390 landscape previews were checked; details are in [verification.md](verification.md). 13. **Refine the character and preview studio.** The shared character now has smoother fabric, connected knee/elbow transitions, a shorter neck and an open jacket with thin cloth lapels over its inner shirt. The preview uses gentler directional lighting, a lavender studio, a two-tier pedestal and a soft radial ground shadow. Explicit **Full body** and **Face & hair** controls replace the single inspect icon; turning and Front view remain available. Framing retains the character and complete pedestal in narrow views while keeping the selected turn/view through appearance edits. The same character improvements reach streets, rooms and transport. All 317 web tests and the final production build pass; existing resource limits are retained. ## Art direction and limits

Original Lisbon-inspired decoration draws on [the Lisbon tourism board's azulejo route](https://www.visitlisboa.com/en/lisbon-stories/18-route-du-azulejo), [the Instituto Superior Técnico's Pombaline façade study](https://fenix.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/departamentos/decivil/dissertacao/2353642135026), [Oriente station](https://www.visitlisboa.com/pt-pt/locais/estacao-oriente) and [LX Factory](https://www.visitlisboa.com/pt-pt/locais/lx-fabricas). Their photographs and assets are not copied into ROAM.

Most source buildings have no detailed architectural tags. The extra colours, window forms and roofs are illustrative and not surveyed reconstructions. Roofs remain inside the source footprint and total height. Close ornament is bounded to 64 nearby buildings per chunk within 350 metres; only three geographic chunks are retained. Detail fades by visibility level, without changing mapped street or building positions.

### Street-life placement and limits

Scenic pedestrians use safe sections of actual mapped walking routes near the camera, with a maximum of 28 people in the scene and an independent 32-profile hard cap. Families count within that cap. Their three members share speed, pauses and direction, retaining their formation through route reversal. Stroller routes must be non-step pedestrian paths at least 2.4 metres wide, with gentle slopes, additional building clearance and four-metre anchor margins. Child proportions apply only to simulated family members; account characters remain adult models. Nearby real players retain separate identity and messaging behavior.

Supplementary street details attach to existing mapped venue façades, using the original POI identity/name. Each chunk allows at most 24 fronts, 24 parasols, 48 planters and 24 menu boards in no more than 13 draw batches. Furniture preserves road widths, a central corridor on wide pedestrian streets, source building/water clearance and terrace reservations. Lantern heights stay below the mounted building's roof. These are original category illustrations rather than verified business exteriors or current menus.

Park paths follow source footways inside source park/wood rings. Each chunk caps path sections at 180, garden benches at 18 and flowerbeds at 24; existing overall landscaping caps remain 420 park trees, 180 roadside trees, 48 benches and 100 lamps. Venue reservations also keep landscaping and walking profiles clear of terraces. Street details regenerate after 120 metres of camera movement; pedestrians recenter after 70 metres. Characters hide and pause in the distant view, and chunk disposal releases instanced buffers. These bounds limit work; they are not physical-device frame-rate measurements.

Screenshots are saved locally under `/private/tmp/roam-city-refresh/`.

These checks do not establish physical-device frame rates, real operator bookings, surveyed building/interior appearance or a hosted staging deployment. Plane and vehicle scenes are original illustrations; the destination camera returns to the geographic world.

## Latest main integration

Muna's `0bdea439` (live map presence) and `2ccd08d2` (customizable saved appearance) are integrated. Chat, account-scoped trip saves, strict request limits and account error handling remain. The two `004` migrations retain their original filenames/checksums; appearance backfill supplies existing accounts before the new API starts. Logout clears presence and revokes the session atomically.

Presence uses one bounded polling chain, rejects stale location results and validates public peer fields. Explicit hide waits for the outstanding heartbeat and confirms deletion; reconnecting peers expire. Polling cannot reset the travel camera or rebuild actors mid-sequence. Saved Male/Female model, outfit, hair, skin, trousers, shoes and top colour carry into the city, rooms and transport traveller. The wardrobe updates the local character in place; a later confirmed journey starts with the newest saved look. Existing appearances default to the male model through additive migration `009_character_body_type.sql`; explicit invalid model choices are rejected.

Beach framing samples actual nearby water and chooses a clear inland camera facing the shoreline. Tests cover all 24 source beaches in desktop and portrait framing, with collision/missing-coast fallbacks. Geographic coordinates and coastline geometry stay unchanged.
