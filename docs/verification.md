## Geographic bundle

The compressed manifest and terrain files were read successfully. The manifest records 273,347 building footprints, 123,185 roads/paths, 16,377 surfaces and 83 chunks. The 86 `.json.gz` files total **19,331,582 bytes (19.33 MB)**. Terrain is a 193 × 193 grid. The OSM source timestamp is 2 October 2026 at 23:00 UTC; acquisition was 7 October 2026. Provenance and the reproducible validator command are in `docs/geography.md`.

The additional uncompressed `lisbon-venues.json` directory is **181,629 bytes**, with 600 named venues selected from 8,757 eligible OSM features: 160 cafés, 80 gyms/studios, 160 restaurants, 90 bars/nightlife venues, 70 shops and 40 cultural venues. Category quotas and geographic-cell sampling preserve coverage beyond central Lisbon. Its source timestamp matches the geography bundle. Every retained record carries its node/way identity and source link; way-centroid positions are explicitly approximate. The directory and geometry share the published ODbL attribution and license in `public/data/LICENSE.txt`.

The uncompressed `lisbon-locations.json` index is **627,553 bytes**, with 1,100 sampled street/path names and 400 mapped place labels. All 1,500 records retain OSM provenance and the same source timestamp. Connected same-name street ways are grouped before sampling; separate regional streets with the same name remain distinct. Street focuses lie on their mapped routes near their centroids and do not represent exact addresses or entrances. The downloadable name index is included in the published ODbL data license.

The bundled world and coordinate-link pins need no Google credential. Google 3D/Places live validation has not been performed because no key is configured. No paid Google requests were made during these checks.
